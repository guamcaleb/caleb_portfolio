Case study: the Select Care campaign

From Instagram comment to enrolled member

I designed and built this funnel for Unified, the gym I own in Guam. A comment on a post starts a ManyChat conversation. The conversation sends people to an enrollment page on our site, and every submission lands with the team. Tap through the six steps to see what each tool does and where a person still has to step in.
